    EMERGENCY INFORMATION
    
Snakebite First Aid

    
Do not wait for snake identification. Seek medical attention immediately.

  

  

    

      
✅ DO

      
    
        
  • Keep the person calm and still.
    • 
        
  • Keep the bitten limb still.
    • 
        
  • Remove rings, watches, and tight items.
    • 
        
  • Arrange urgent transport to a hospital.
    • 
        
  • Remember the snake's appearance only if safe.
    • 
      

    

    

      
❌ DON'T

      
    
        
  • Do not suck out venom.
    • 
        
  • Do not cut or burn the wound.
    • 
        
  • Do not apply a tight tourniquet.
    • 
        
  • Do not try to catch the snake.
    • 
        
  • Do not delay medical treatment.
    • 
      

    

  

  

    
Snakebite Emergency
Do not wait for AI identification. Get professional medical help immediately.

    📞 Call 108
