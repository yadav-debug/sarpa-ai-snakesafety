EMERGENCY INFORMATION

Snakebite First Aid

If someone has been bitten, do not wait for snake identification. Seek medical attention immediately.

✅ DO

  • Keep the person calm.
  • Keep the bitten limb still.
  • Remove rings, watches and tight items.
  • Reach a hospital immediately.
  • Remember the snake's appearance only if safe.

❌ DON'T

  • Do not suck the venom.
  • Do not cut the wound.
  • Do not apply a tight tourniquet.
  • Do not try to catch the snake.
  • Do not delay hospital treatment.

Snakebite Emergency

Do not wait for AI identification. Get professional medical help immediately.

📞 Call 108
