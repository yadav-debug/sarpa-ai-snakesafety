            EMERGENCY INFORMATION
            
What to Do After a Snakebite

            
Do not wait for identification or symptoms to become severe before seeking medical care.

        


        

            

                01
                
Move away

                
Get away from the snake without trying to catch or kill it.

            

            

                02
                
Stay calm and still

                
Limit movement and keep the bitten limb as still as practical.

            

            

                03
                
Remove tight items

                
Remove rings, watches or tight items near the affected area before swelling develops, if easy to do.

            

            

                04
                
Get medical help

                
Arrange urgent transport to a hospital. Follow emergency professionals' instructions.

            

        


        

            Do not cut or suck the wound, apply ice or a tight tourniquet, use electric shocks, or try traditional remedies. Do not delay hospital treatment.
